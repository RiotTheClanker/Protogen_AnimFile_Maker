## Protogen Animation Tool V4

Downloads: **Windows** `Windows_protogen_tool.exe` · **macOS** `Mac_protogen_tool` · **Linux** `Linux_protogen_tool`

### Fixes
- **The window fits on the screen.** It used to open at the full size of its contents, which on
  1080p or scaled (high-DPI) screens ran past the bottom edge and hid controls such as
  Open and Save. It now opens sized to the screen, can be resized, and the LED grid scales with it.

Works with ProtoFace **V6.2**. The `.anim` file format is unchanged.
