Draw for any display ProtoFace 7.0 supports (HUB75, MAX7219, custom NeoPixel boards) from its display.json, with a new built-in help guide.

## Protogen Animation Tool V5

Downloads: **Windows** `Windows_protogen_tool.exe` · **macOS** `Mac_protogen_tool` · **Linux** `Linux_protogen_tool`

Made for ProtoFace **V7.0**. There's also a browser version, the **Animation Maker** on the website,
alongside the **Display Builder** for making a `display.json`.

### New
- **Any display.** Pick Mk2 or Mk3 in the toolbar, or **Open display.json…** to draw for a
  HUB75 panel, MAX7219 8×8 matrices or a custom NeoPixel PCB. Use the same `display.json`
  that is on the helmet's SD card (the presets and examples are attached to the ProtoFace V7.0 release).
- **Help guide** (F1) with live demos of pixels, sound reaction and frames, plus tooltips,
  keyboard shortcuts and undo (Ctrl+Z).

### Compatibility
- `.anim` files for Mk2 / Mk3 keep the old format, so they still play on V6.2 as well as V7.0.
- Files for other displays use a new version 2 header that needs ProtoFace V7.0.

### Known
- **Export .h** makes the old built-in fallback face, which ProtoFace V7.0 no longer uses.
